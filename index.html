Profile

A practitioner building towards a career in cybersecurity through deliberate, hands-on work not theory alone.

I am a cybersecurity analyst in development, focused on vulnerability management and security operations. My approach is grounded in real lab environments, actual enterprise tooling, and the specific workflows that matter to hiring managers, not certification cramming for its own sake.

My current work centres on building a structured lab around Windows security telemetry: Sysmon log analysis, PowerShell endpoint investigation, and CVE research mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Every project I build is documented with the precision I would bring to a real SOC role.

I study for CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 with the same rigour, building custom study tooling, drilling interview-ready explanations of technical concepts, and practising the kind of nuanced articulation that separates analysts who understand their work from those who merely pass exams.
